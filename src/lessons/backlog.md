title: Catching up after time away
group: scheduling
evidence: fsrs
summary: Hundreds of overdue cards? Stop new cards, cap reviews, and start with what you still know.

After a holiday or a busy month the overdue cards pile up. Trying to clear them in one day is tiring and often ends with giving up.

## Catch-up mode

AiNKI offers catch-up mode when many cards are overdue. You can also turn it on in settings. While it is on:

- No new cards. Each new card adds about ten reviews in its first month. They can wait.
- A daily cap on reviews, 100 by default, across all decks.
- The cards you most likely still remember come first.

## Why those first?

It sounds backwards. But a card you have probably forgotten is lost already, and reviewing it today or next week costs about the same. A card you still remember is at risk: each day it waits, the chance grows that it slips too. Reviewing those first saves the most memories for the time you have. Simulations by the FSRS community on real review data agree.

## When you are through

AiNKI tells you on the home page when the backlog is gone. Turn catch-up mode off, and new cards come back.
