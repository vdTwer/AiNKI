title: The spacing effect
group: memory
evidence: strong
summary: The same study time, spread out, is remembered far longer.

Study something three times in one evening and you will know it the next morning. Study it three times over three weeks and you will still know it months later. This is the spacing effect. It is one of the most reliable findings in the psychology of learning.

## What the research says

Hundreds of experiments, with words, facts, skills, children and adults, find the same thing: spreading study out beats cramming for anything you want to keep. Cramming can win for a test tomorrow. It loses badly a week later.

The best gap depends on how long you want to remember. The longer the goal, the longer the gaps should be. Spaced repetition does this for you: each successful review stretches the next gap.

## For you

- A little every day works better than a lot on one day. Stats show on how many days you studied this week.
- Don't review cards early to feel safe. The gap is what does the work.
- Missing a day is fine. FSRS adjusts.
