title: Interference
group: memory
evidence: strong
summary: Similar memories compete. Make cards that are easy to tell apart.

Much forgetting is not fading. It is competition. When two memories are alike, recalling one brings up the other. Old learning can block new learning, and new learning can block old. Psychologists call this interference, and it has been studied for over a century.

## Where it bites

- Words that look or sound alike: affect and effect, or embarazada and embarrassed.
- Two cards with nearly the same question and different answers.
- Facts learned in a rush from several similar sources.

## How AiNKI helps

While you write a card, AiNKI looks for cards with a very similar front and tells you. Cards that look like another card are also listed under cards that need a look.

## What to do

- Make the fronts differ in what matters. Add the context that decides the answer.
- Learn similar items together, and add a card that asks for the difference.
- Learn one of the pair well first, then add the other.
