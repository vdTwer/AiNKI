title: Images and mnemonics
group: cards
evidence: moderate
summary: Pictures and memory hooks help, with a few catches.

## Pictures

People remember pictures better than words. This picture superiority effect is well established. A card with an image is also easier to tell apart from similar cards. Images work best when they show what the card is about: a map, a diagram, the face of the person. A decorative stock photo adds little.

Each side of an AiNKI card can have one image.

## Mnemonics

A mnemonic is a hook you make up to link a fact to something you already know. For vocabulary the keyword method is the best known: link the sound of the new word to a familiar word, then to the meaning, with a vivid image. The Spanish pato means duck: picture a duck sitting in a pot.

Studies find the keyword method speeds up early learning. Some find it is forgotten faster later than plain study, and it takes time to invent good hooks. Use mnemonics for the cards that won't stick, not for everything.

## For you

- Add an image when the card is about something you can see.
- Write a mnemonic on the back of a card that keeps slipping.
- Funny, odd and personal hooks work better than plain ones.
