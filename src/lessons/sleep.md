title: Sleep and memory
group: memory
evidence: moderate
summary: Sleep helps new memories settle. Studying late is fine; staying up isn't.

Memories are not finished when you close the app. During sleep, especially deep sleep, the brain replays and stabilizes what it learned during the day. People who sleep after learning remember more than people who stay awake for the same time.

## What the research says

The benefit of sleep after learning is well documented. Some studies also find that studying in the evening, shortly before sleep, helps a little more than studying in the morning. That finding is less certain, and there is no good evidence for a best time of day in general.

What is clear: losing sleep to study more is a bad trade.

## For you

- Study when it suits you.
- Don't cut sleep for reviews. FSRS can wait a day.
- If late cards keep you up, set quiet hours in settings. AiNKI then shows a rest screen instead of cards, and agents won't quiz you.
