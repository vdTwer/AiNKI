title: Cards that keep slipping
group: scheduling
evidence: practice
summary: Leeches cost a lot of time. Fixing the card usually beats reviewing it again.

A leech is a card you keep forgetting. Each lapse sends it back to short gaps, so a few leeches can take more time than hundreds of easy cards.

## How AiNKI spots them

A card is flagged as one that keeps slipping when you have forgotten it 5 times and its memory still lasts under 60 days. AiNKI only flags it. It never suspends a card by itself. Flagged cards show on the card page, while studying, and in the list of cards that need a look.

## What to do

Most leeches have a cause in the card, not in you. Ask:

- Is it one fact? A card that asks for several things fails when any one is missing. [Split it](/learn/lists).
- Is the question clear? If more than one answer fits, you will often give the other one.
- Is it like another card? Similar cards get mixed up. See [interference](/learn/interference). Make them differ, or add what tells them apart.
- Do you understand it? Facts without meaning are hard to keep. Learn the idea first, then make cards.
- Would a picture or a hook help? See [images and mnemonics](/learn/images-and-mnemonics).

If none of this works and the card doesn't matter much, suspend it. That is a fine outcome.
