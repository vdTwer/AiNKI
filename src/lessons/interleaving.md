title: Interleaving
group: memory
evidence: mixed
summary: Mixing similar topics helps you tell them apart. Mixing unrelated ones is just convenient.

Interleaving means mixing different kinds of items in one session instead of doing them in blocks. A, B, C, A, C, B instead of A, A, A, B, B, B.

## When it helps

Interleaving helps most when the items are easy to confuse and the task is to tell them apart. Students who saw paintings by several artists mixed together were better at naming the artist of new paintings. Students who practiced mixed types of math problems were better at picking the right method.

A large meta-analysis found a clear benefit for such material. For word lists and texts it found little or none.

## Mixing decks

There is no good evidence that mixing unrelated decks, such as Spanish and anatomy, helps you learn either one. AiNKI lets you study all decks at once because it is convenient: one session, no choosing. It is not a learning trick.

## For you

- When cards are easy to mix up, keep them in one deck so they come up together.
- Study all decks at once if you like. Study one deck at a time if you prefer. Both are fine.
